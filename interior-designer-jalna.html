Can you help with renovating an old house?

Yes. Send photos and rough measurements of the existing rooms. I redesign the layout, kitchen, bedrooms, ceiling and lighting, and show the before-and-after in 3D so you can decide what to change and what to keep.

Why should I see my interior in 3D first?

A 3D view shows the exact colours, laminates, tiles, furniture sizes and lighting before you buy material or pay for labour. You can change things on screen instead of breaking and rebuilding on site, which saves money and avoids arguments with contractors.
